38 of 412 reports carry an intake tag that does not match where they are filed.

  • parser — the shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so 7 April comes out as 4 July.
  • hint — the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and then reads the date day first. People do what the hint says, so their date flips. The parser fix does not touch this form.
  • shift — some dates did not flip. They moved one day earlier, and people in different places saw different days for the same meeting. That is a time zone bug. The desk files these reports under parser by tenant, so they count in both rows.

Why one ticket hid three

  • We merged on subject line (decision 0001). All three problems produce the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”), so the subject cannot tell them apart.
  • Intake tags come from the reporter’s browser, not the tenant. Many reports carry the wrong tag, so the tags cannot tell them apart either.
  • The kb’s own table below found the hint bug and the one-day moves, then marked the first “no ticket of its own” and put the second in a footnote under parser.
