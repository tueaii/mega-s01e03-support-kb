Closed 5 October. The parser is fixed. The booking-v3 form is retired, so its hint will not be fixed.

5 reports read as a one-day move; counted under parser by tenant.

  • parser — the shared parser read every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so 7 April came out as 4 July. Fixed in fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.
  • hint — the booking-v3 form printed MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and then read the date day first. People did what the hint said, so their date flipped. The parser fix did not touch this form. The form is retired, so this one closes as won’t fix.

Why one ticket hid two

  • We merged on subject line (decision 0001). Both problems produce the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”), so the subject cannot tell them apart.
  • Intake tags come from the reporter’s browser, not the tenant, so they cannot tell them apart either. The cause comes from the tenant: its locale and its booking form.
  • The kb’s own cause table found the hint problem and marked it “no ticket of its own”. Nobody opened one.

What to do next time

  1. Before a big ticket is marked known, read its cause table. A second row is a second ticket.
  2. Write a new decision record to replace 0001: merge on cause (tenant locale, booking form, channel), not on subject.
